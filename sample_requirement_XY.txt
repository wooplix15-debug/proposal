1. Company Background
XY Manufacturing Private Limited is an India-based manufacturer and distributor of industrial equipment.
The company has approximately 85 office users across Sales, Finance, Operations, Service and Management,
with field sales and service teams working from multiple locations.
2. Current Business Process
The company currently uses a combination of spreadsheets, email, shared folders and separate applications for
different functions. Accounting is handled in Tally Prime. Sales enquiries are mostly tracked through
spreadsheets and email. Inventory information is maintained in the accounting system and separate Excel
files. Service requests are received through email and phone and are manually assigned to service personnel.
Management does not have a single real-time dashboard covering the sales pipeline, receivables, inventory
status, service performance and operational KPIs.
3. Business Challenges
 Lead and enquiry tracking is inconsistent and depends heavily on individual sales employees.
 There is no standard sales pipeline with defined stages, activities and follow-up reminders.
 Customer and contact information is duplicated across spreadsheets.
 Quotation and sales follow-up status is difficult for management to monitor.
 Finance users spend significant time reconciling information between Tally, spreadsheets and emails.
 Inventory availability is not easily visible to sales teams while preparing quotations.
 Customer service requests are not tracked through a standard ticketing process.
 Management reporting requires manual consolidation from multiple sources.
 The company wants to reduce manual data entry and improve process visibility.
4. Project Objectives
 Create a centralized customer and sales management system.
 Standardize lead-to-customer and quotation follow-up processes.
 Improve visibility of sales activities and pipeline value.
 Improve coordination between Sales, Finance, Operations and Service.
 Automate routine notifications and follow-up tasks.
 Provide management dashboards and KPI reporting.
 Integrate relevant business data with the existing accounting environment.
 Reduce duplicate data entry and spreadsheet-based reporting.
5. Functional Requirements
5.1 Sales & CRM
 Capture leads from website forms, email enquiries and manual entry.
 Maintain accounts, contacts and customer history.
 Define a sales pipeline with configurable stages.
 Assign leads and opportunities to sales users based on territory or business rules.
 Create tasks, calls and follow-up reminders.

 Track opportunity value, expected closing date and probability.
 Generate quotations and track quotation status.
 Convert qualified leads into customers without duplicate data.
 Maintain a record of customer interactions and sales notes.
5.2 Finance & Order Information
 Sales users should be able to view relevant customer financial information where permitted.
 Users should be able to see invoice and outstanding information for customers.
 The solution should reduce manual re-entry of customer and transaction information between systems.
 Finance should be able to reconcile customer and order information against accounting records.
 The implementation partner should assess whether the existing Tally environment can be integrated
directly or through a suitable middleware/API/file-based method.
5.3 Inventory & Operations
 Maintain product/item master data with SKU, product name, category, unit and selling price.
 Provide sales users with visibility of stock availability where feasible.
 Track stock movements and relevant inventory transactions.
 Generate low-stock or reorder alerts based on agreed business rules.
 Provide management reporting on stock availability and inventory value.
5.4 Customer Service
 Allow customers or internal users to raise service requests.
 Assign tickets to support/service employees.
 Track ticket priority, category, status and resolution time.
 Send acknowledgement and status notifications.
 Maintain service history against the customer record.
 Provide service performance reporting for management.
5.5 Analytics & Management Reporting
 Sales pipeline dashboard by salesperson, territory and stage.
 Lead source and conversion analysis.
 Quotation and opportunity ageing.
 Sales performance by month, product and salesperson.
 Receivables / outstanding summary by customer.
 Inventory availability and low-stock reporting.
 Service ticket volume, ageing and resolution performance.
 Executive dashboard with key business KPIs.
6. Automation Requirements
 Automatic lead assignment based on defined rules.
 Follow-up reminders for sales opportunities and quotations.
 Notifications when opportunities remain inactive beyond a defined period.
 Customer communication triggered by agreed sales/service events.
 Approval workflow for discounts above authorized limits.
 Alerts for low stock or selected inventory thresholds.
 Escalation notifications for service tickets approaching SLA limits.
 Scheduled management reports.

7. Integration Requirements
 Existing accounting platform: Tally Prime.
 Company website lead/enquiry forms.
 Company email for enquiry and notification flows.
 Optional consideration of WhatsApp or another messaging channel for customer communication; final
scope to be confirmed.
 The implementation partner should recommend the appropriate integration approach and identify any
API, middleware or file-exchange dependency.
8. Users, Roles & Access
Initial user estimate: approximately 85 users.
 Sales Team: 35 users.
 Finance Team: 10 users.
 Operations / Inventory: 15 users.
 Service Team: 15 users.
 Management / Admin: 10 users.
The solution should support role-based permissions so that users can access only the information relevant to
their responsibilities. Management should have cross-functional reporting visibility.
9. Data Migration Requirements
 Migrate customer/account records from existing spreadsheets.
 Migrate contact records and available customer history where practical.
 Migrate product/item master data.
 Migrate open opportunities and quotations if technically feasible.
 Define data cleansing and deduplication rules before migration.
 Provide a migration validation/reconciliation process.
10. Proposed Implementation Expectations
 Implementation partner to conduct a detailed discovery workshop before final solution design.
 Configuration and customization should be based on approved process maps.
 Integration approach should be documented before development begins.
 The solution should be tested in a non-production environment before go-live.
 User Acceptance Testing should be completed with designated business users.
 Partner should provide administrator and end-user training.
 Partner should provide solution documentation and a basic operating guide.
11. Security, Audit & Governance
 Role-based access controls are required.
 Important record changes should be traceable where supported by the selected platform.
 Administrative access should be restricted to authorized personnel.
 Integration credentials and secrets should not be stored in plain text.
